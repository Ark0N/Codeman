# Codeman media kit (October 2026)

Lossless screenshots of a live Codeman 1.40.0 with real agent sessions (Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, Pi, DeepSeek Harness on a local GPU). Every terminal is real output; session and project names are invented. Free to use for articles, posts and talks about Codeman (MIT project; credit "Codeman, getcodeman.com" appreciated).

`masters/` are the full-resolution PNGs (desktop 1600x900 CSS at 2x or 3x, phone at 3x). `crops/` are README-width (2400 px) versions, a phone at 780 px, and two 1280x640 social cards. `index.png` is a contact sheet of everything.
