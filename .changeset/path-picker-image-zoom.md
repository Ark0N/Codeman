The path picker's image preview can now be zoomed: pinch and drag on a phone, the mouse wheel or a trackpad on a desktop, double-click to toggle 2x, and zoom out / fit / zoom in buttons. A small image is shown at its own size instead of being stretched to fill the preview. Thanks to @shenlvkang-collab, whose #608 this comes from.
