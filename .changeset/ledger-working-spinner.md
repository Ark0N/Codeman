The Ledger tab layout shows the spinning ring on a working session again. The ledger swaps the status dot for a coloured bar, and that bar is green for idle and working alike, so hiding the dot also hid the only sign that a session was busy. A working cell now keeps the dot and its ring, the same mark the classic strip draws; idle cells stay bar-only, and an exited agent's cell still shows no ring.
