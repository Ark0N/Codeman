Drag the line between the session tabs and the header toolbar to resize the toolbar. Dragging it right narrows the toolbar, which then wraps onto a second line instead of squeezing out the tabs, and the usage readings shed their labels, then their provider names, then their percentages as it narrows; double-click, Home or Escape resets it. Arrow keys resize it from the keyboard, and the width is remembered on the device.
