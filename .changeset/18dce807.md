Config backups: timestamped snapshots of the files that hold your customization (settings, CLI overrides, custom endpoints, webhook, linked cases and, in multi-user mode, accounts), taken at startup and whenever one changes. On by default into `~/.codeman/backups/config`; the folder and the retention (keep N backups, drop older than N days) are configurable in Settings → System, with Back up now and Restore. Retention only deletes folders Codeman made, never the newest backup.
